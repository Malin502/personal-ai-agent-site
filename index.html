From messages to meaningful actions.

Email triage

Self-hosted Gmail integration that checks new inbox mail every 30 minutes, flags actionable messages, and proposes replies.

▦

Calendar assistance

Read-only integration that reviews accessible calendars and summarizes schedules in Japan time.

✓

Human oversight

Approval-first workflows for outgoing email and other consequential operations.

Personal, not a public service.

This project is intended for its developer's own use. It does not offer public account registration or access to other users' Google data. Google API integrations are private and enabled only for the developer; automated sending and calendar modifications are not enabled.
